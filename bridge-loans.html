When financing and timing do not line up

A bridge loan needs a clear destination.

Temporary financing can create a connection between two events. Start by identifying the gap, the property involved, and exactly how you expect the loan to be repaid.

The purpose of the bridge changes the conversation

Bridge financing is generally temporary financing tied to a later event, such as a sale or a refinance. An investor's property transaction and a homeowner's plan to buy before selling can have different program and borrower requirements. Explain which situation you are in before comparing options; the name alone does not establish eligibility.

Who this conversation helps

An investment purchase

You are evaluating temporary financing while another source of funds or a longer-term loan is being arranged.

A property in transition

A sale, renovation, or rental plan affects when you expect to repay or refinance the existing debt.

A move with overlapping dates

You need to discuss buying and selling timelines. Logan can first determine whether a homeowner financing route is available for the situation.

What we’ll discuss

  • What event creates the need for temporary funds, and which dates are contractual rather than preferred?
  • Which property would secure the loan, and what debts or liens already exist?
  • Will you occupy any property involved, or is the transaction for business-purpose investment?
  • How will you cover payments, insurance, taxes, and other costs while the properties or loans overlap?
  • What is the repayment event, what evidence supports it, and what is the backup if it is delayed?

Information to bring

  • The addresses, current ownership, and intended use of each property involved.
  • Existing loan balances and available mortgage or lien information.
  • Purchase contracts, listing information, or a concise explanation of the planned transaction.
  • A timeline showing the funds needed and the anticipated sale or refinance.
  • A summary of your available cash and the costs you expect to carry during the overlap.

Test the plan against a longer timeline

Review the interest, fees, payment schedule, maturity, and extension provisions together. Ask how a delayed sale or refinance would affect your cash needs and what happens when the loan becomes due. An extension should never be assumed.

Property equity does not automatically become available borrowing capacity. The lender must evaluate value, existing liens, loan structure, borrower qualifications, and the proposed use of funds. Confirm which property or properties would secure the debt.

A future mortgage requires its own approval. If refinancing is the expected exit, discuss the likely documentation and property conditions now, while recognizing that future terms and eligibility may change.

How the conversation moves forward

  1. Map the gap

    Identify the two events, the properties, the amount of temporary funding needed, and the actual deadlines.

  2. Compare a workable structure

    Review any available options alongside timing alternatives, including cash contribution, collateral, carrying costs, and loan obligations.

  3. Confirm the exit and conditions

    Discuss how repayment is expected to happen and the backup plan. A lender must complete its review before financing can be relied upon.

Common questions

Are investor bridge loans and buy-before-you-sell loans the same?

No. Property use, loan purpose, collateral, and borrower requirements can differ. Tell Logan whether the transaction involves your home or an investment so the appropriate options can be reviewed.

Can a bridge loan stay in place if my sale is delayed?

Only the actual loan terms can answer that. Ask about maturity, any available extension process and cost, and the lender's requirements. Do not build your plan around an unconfirmed extension.

Does bridge financing guarantee a later refinance?

No. A later lender will evaluate the application, property, and program requirements at that time. Review a repayment alternative before relying on refinancing as the only exit.
