## Decisions already reflected in the draft

- Each page has substantive static content: a compact introduction, relevant situations, discussion questions, information to prepare, terms and constraints to review, process, FAQs, adjacent links, and a scenario-aware contact CTA.
- The DSCR page separates eligible rent and a lender-defined qualifying payment from investment profitability. It does not publish a universal ratio threshold or promise that credit, cash, reserves, or other borrower review disappear.
- Consumer home financing and business-purpose investment financing are distinguished. The Non-QM page does not imply that all rental loans belong to the same consumer mortgage category.
- Construction-to-permanent, standalone home equity loans, homeowner bridge structures, short-term rental qualification, and alternative jumbo documentation are questions to confirm, not asserted universal offerings.
- No land product page is added. A construction page question about a site is not evidence of a separate land loan.
- No rates, APR examples, minimum scores, leverage limits, loan sizes, minimum statement periods, fixed funding timelines, guaranteed approvals, endorsements, production totals, client stories, or specific lender terms are introduced.
- No claims of direct lending, proprietary capital, in-house underwriting, or lender reach are added.
- The public contact route requests a general situation. The bank statement FAQ directs detailed financial records to the appropriate secure application instructions rather than the marketing inquiry form.
- New footers retain the protected IDs exactly: Logan Sullivan NMLS 2466872 and Forward Loans NMLS 2006640. At the implementation lead's direction, preview legal identity is `Forward Holdings LLC dba Forward Loans`. The existing Forward biography URL is labeled `Company profile`, not an NMLS registry record.
- Source references and pending facts stay in internal metadata. Customer-facing content has no approval placeholders or development notes.
- Each page has one `<!-- LOGAN_VIDEOS -->` insertion point. There are no video cards or fabricated media. The implementation lead owns the approved-video renderer and publishing conditions.
